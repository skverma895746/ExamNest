Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account to take a test?
No. Every test is open to anyone — just pick one from Get Test and click Start.
Will I lose my progress if I close the tab?
Your answers are auto-saved as you go, but a fresh timer starts if you restart a test from scratch.
How is my score calculated?
Each test's negative marking rules are applied automatically — correct, wrong, and unattempted answers are shown separately in your results.
Can I retake a test?
Yes, anytime. Your browser remembers your last score so you can track improvement across attempts.
Who manages the tests?
A single administrator uploads, reviews and publishes every test through a secured dashboard.
